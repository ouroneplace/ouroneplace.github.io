## Truth, privacy, safety and limits

The Saturday center, notices, hours and messages are entirely fictional. This page provides no actual local legal requirement, service guarantee, operator assignment or response timetable. Local public offices and community providers vary by region. A visitor should independently verify the current official channel; do not enter a dangerous area, confront others, share sensitive details, or use a general message for emergencies. Use local emergency services for immediate danger.

Four textareas are capped at 320 characters each. No account, payment, booking, geolocation, analytics, cookies, local/session storage, outgoing network requests, personal details or message submission. Text exists only in the active page and disappears on reload. Scored assessments are not used. Changing a checked response invalidates old feedback; blank choices are not marked reviewed. Example load confirms before overwriting nonempty text, and full clear confirms before losing any written or selected work, including unreviewed answers. Cancel preserves entries and state; an empty reset needs no dialog.

A script-disabled visitor can still read the five checks, fictional answers and expanded complete paper request; script-only dead controls are hidden. There are accessible fieldset legends, four textarea labels, live status feedback, keyboard focus, a focusable main target for skip navigation, reduced-motion-compatible behavior and responsive layout.

The official USAGov state and local government directory link (https://www.usa.gov/state-local-governments) is an opt-in general starting point already listed in the accepted Community catalog. It is not a guarantee of live local contact details; external uptime and specific applicability remain unverified.
