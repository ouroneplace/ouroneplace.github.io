## Verified gap and non-duplication
Current published main includes the Mental Health & Safety catalog, live first-party `one-place/support-skills-safety.html` (four supporting-**others** practice situations), and general grounding/coping starting links. It does NOT have a first-party, self-directed, optional everyday stress practice. This proposal fills that narrow difference. Existing support lab and all accepted public files stay byte-identical. No clinical screening or crisis intake.

## Visitor outcome
Three fictional scenarios teach:
1. Present-moment sensory noticing with permission to stop or choose an accessible alternative.
2. A specific, consensual support request and backup when another person is unavailable.
3. Recognizing persistent/worsening interference and contacting qualified professional care.
All include WHY, a low-pressure HOW, common mistake, clear safety STOP and three unscored response options. Correct/wrong/blank responses give explanatory feedback; changing a choice invalidates prior feedback; reset starts again. Without JS, all instructions and suggested safe options remain plain text. Print, navigation and 320px/200%-text are considered. No timers, forced breathing, private text fields, risk/diagnosis score, alerts, logins, uploads, cookies or persisted data.
