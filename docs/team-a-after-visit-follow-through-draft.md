## Real visitor outcome and safe boundaries
- 3 original, plain-language WHY/SCENARIO/HOW/MISTAKE/STOP lessons with 9 fictional answer-specific reasons; no named medicines/values.
- 3-step follow-through: use verified clinic contacts, ask who owns the result/referral/clarification, confirm when and how the right person will respond.
- Optional local-only question-wording builder: topic, contact, confirmation, invalidates stale preview, manual text selection, reset; **not sent, not a medical record**.
- No patient names, dates, diagnoses, birth dates, portal credentials, lab values, prescriptions, uploads, real URLs, browser storage or network POST. Static teaching and sample sentence stay readable without JavaScript. Do not assume silence means normal results, any referral guarantees an appointment, or a quiz can change a dose.
- Clear urgent warning above activities: for severe/new/worsening symptoms seek timely clinical advice or local emergency help as appropriate. Only the treating team can choose clinical next steps. U.S. contextual sources; local care access and insurance vary.
