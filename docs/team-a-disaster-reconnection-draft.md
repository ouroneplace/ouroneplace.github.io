## Original visitor outcomes
1. A person facing failed phone calls can try agreed text and non-phone backup without trusting a queued/unanswered message as confirmation; real danger -> live help.
2. Separated household members distinguish a planned nearby versus out-of-neighborhood meeting option, rely on a consenting out-of-area contact, and **never visit a dangerous/closed/flooded destination despite a paper plan**.
3. Caregiving, child release/school, accessibility, older adults and pets have verified authorized and private plans, not improvised transfer or public posting of names/location.
4. A nontransmitting discussion outline builder offers THREE predefined categorical inputs (need, backup, safety check), a read-only manually selectable generic text response, edit invalidation, reset, and a full no-JavaScript paper outline. No personal data fields; page asks visitors NOT to type real names, addresses, phone numbers, medical/child details or passwords. No automatic request, email, background fetching, browser storage or upload. Nine option-specific reasons exposed in an accessible `details` element even when JavaScript is off.

## Official corroboration directly reviewed Nov 14, 2026
- CDC, Stay Connected (published April 18 2024): https://www.cdc.gov/prepare-your-health/plan-ahead/stay-connected.html — two meeting places, multiple communication methods, out-of-area contacts, children, caregivers and pets.
- American Red Cross, Make a Plan: https://www.redcross.org/get-help/how-to-prepare-for-emergencies/make-a-plan.html — before-event family and out-of-area contact, two meeting sites, keeping written copies and responding to current risks.
- American Red Cross, Contact Loved Ones: https://www.redcross.org/get-help/disaster-relief-and-recovery-services/contact-and-locate-loved-ones.html — possible additional help when separated, subject to criteria; **no guaranteed availability or identity/location verification**.
All external URLs are opt-in no scripts; current specific official alerts/school/transport rules outrank the fictional lesson. No individual emergency route or reunification assistance/monitoring offered.
