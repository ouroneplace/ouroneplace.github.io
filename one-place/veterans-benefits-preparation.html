Disability compensation

Prepare a claim, new condition, or request for an increased rating.

Why this matters: VA looks at the claim type, service connection and available evidence. An intent to file may help protect a potential effective date for certain claims, but is not a claim approval.

Important limitation: An intent to file applies to specified claims, not every VA benefit. Check the current rules, forms and deadlines directly. Do not share medical records here.

Learn the difference between a Certificate of Eligibility and loan approval.

Why this matters: A VA Certificate of Eligibility (COE) indicates potential program entitlement, not guaranteed lender underwriting, property approval or an affordable payment.

Important limitation: VA loan rules are U.S.-specific. Housing cost, lender standards, credit, income, occupancy and other requirements still apply. Never treat a calculator or COE as approval.

3. Check for benefit-related scams


Protect your information
  • Verify a representative using VA's official accreditation search before sharing sensitive information.
  • Accredited VSO claim help is free. Do not pay anyone to file an initial VA benefits claim.
  • Be skeptical of guaranteed ratings, expedited approval or pressure to sign immediately.
  • Start at the official VA.gov website, not a link in an unexpected message.


Ask before signing
  1. Who exactly are you, and are you VA-accredited?
  2. What services are free? Are any fees allowed in this type of case?
  3. May I review the full agreement without pressure?
  4. Can I verify the claim with VA independently?

4. Get an official answer, not a guessed outcome


A sensible sequence is to identify the program, read its current criteria, locate relevant records, ask an accredited representative or VA what is still needed, and only then use the official application or review channel. If you have a decision letter or an urgent deadline, prioritize its instructions immediately.


VA military records guidance (external) explains that VA generally requests a DD214 for a VA benefits application; you do not always need to obtain it separately first.


VA decision review guide (external) explains different review choices and timing. Do not apply a general deadline to your specific letter without checking it.


For immediate danger in the United States, call 911. For a Veteran in emotional crisis, call 988 and press 1. These services are separate from this educational page.
