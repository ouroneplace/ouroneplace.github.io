01 · UNDERSTAND COVERAGE

Explore health insurance options

What the terms mean

Premium: the recurring amount paid to keep coverage. Deductible: what you may pay for covered services before a plan starts paying its share, subject to exceptions. Copayment: a set payment for a covered service. Provider network: clinicians and facilities with particular plan arrangements.

Do not compare plans by monthly premiums alone; examine your likely total costs, participating clinicians, prescriptions, benefits and plan documents.

How to check your next step

  1. Identify your coverage path (employer, Medicare, Medicaid, Marketplace or other).
  2. Write down the care, prescriptions, providers and accessibility accommodations that matter to you.
  3. Check plan documents and official enrollment pages directly. Costs, eligibility and deadlines depend on your circumstances.

02 · LEARN THE SKILL

Prepare for a healthcare appointment

Before the visit

  1. Decide your main concern and two or three questions.
  2. Note when changes began, how they affect daily life and what you have already tried, if relevant.
  3. Bring an accurate list of medicines, supplements and allergies to discuss with your care team. Do not change doses based on this tool.
  4. Ask ahead about an interpreter, accessibility or support person if needed.

During and after

  1. Ask the clinician what the recommendation means, which alternatives exist and what risks or benefits matter.
  2. Repeat instructions in your own words and ask for a written summary if helpful.
  3. Before leaving, confirm next steps, how and when results arrive, who to contact and warning signs that require prompt care.
  4. Use a patient portal or office phone for personalized medical questions.

Example questions: “What are the options?” “What happens if I wait?” “What symptoms should make me seek urgent help?” “How will we know whether the plan is working?”
