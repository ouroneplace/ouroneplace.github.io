02 · LEARN HOME SAFETY ESSENTIALS

Small checks. Important protections.

Review the essentials without attempting hazardous repairs. Check an item only after reviewing it; these checkmarks are for your own temporary checklist, not a safety certification.

Respond to immediate hazards first

  • If you smell gas, suspect carbon monoxide, see a fire, or face immediate danger, leave if safe and contact emergency services or the utility's emergency line from a safe location. Do not operate switches or investigate suspected gas leaks yourself.
  • Never run generators, grills, or other fuel-burning equipment indoors. CDC advises keeping generators outdoors at least 20 feet from doors, windows, and vents.
  • Do not touch downed wires or attempt electrical, structural, or gas repairs beyond your qualifications.
  • For an urgent housing-safety or accessibility need, seek qualified local assistance. A checklist cannot certify a home as safe.
